How this game was made

  • Each QB's line is his median from the model's 25,000 simulated games (the same run as the saved projections, 2026-11-05 23:57 UTC). Rushers show their own medians.
  • Receivers all sit at the same point of their own ranges, chosen so they add up exactly to their QB's median (every completion goes to a named player). Why not each receiver's own median: receiving yards are lopsided (most games modest, a few big), so adding every receiver's median comes up short of the team total; in a typical game one or two of them have a big day. Each receiver's own median and his prop odds are in the lines file and the bet finder.
  • The final score is the median score from the model's score simulation, anchored to the market line (JAX −9.5, total 40). JAX 25, BAL 16 came up in 23 of 25,000 sims.
  • Filled in around the model's numbers, because the model does not simulate them: the order of plays, drives, downs, field position, the clock, kicks, and how each player's yards split across his touches. No sacks, penalties or fumbles. Every play is checked so the final box score matches exactly.
